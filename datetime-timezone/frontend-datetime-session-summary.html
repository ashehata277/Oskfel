One date-time field for every date input

<app-date-time-field> is a standalone form control around the Material datepicker. The user always picks from the calendar; typed text is not read. It replaced every Material datepicker and native date / datetime-local input, 90 places in all. The hidden mcr/mor anchor pickers and the date-range inputs are left as they are.

InputValuesJob
modedate · dateTimedateTime adds a time box next to the day.
valueAsdate · day · localDateTimeThe form value's shape: a team Date (or API date-time text), yyyy-MM-dd, or yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm. The field reads and writes only that shape.
lookfill · outline · plainMaterial fill or outline, or a bare input for the custom form layouts.

Regex removal

No date or time regex is left in Frontend, HandoverApp or InspectionsApp. The only regex in the area is SORT_CODE_PATTERN in helpers/custom-validators.ts, which has nothing to do with dates.

WasNow
API_STRING_PATTERN, offsetTextToMsparseApiString: a T at index 10, ends with Z or ±hh:mm, then new Date. The backend's UtcDateTimeJsonConverter sends every date-time with Z on the team-zone branches.
CALENDAR_DAY_PATTERNparseCalendarDay: split on -, parts of 4, 2 and 2 digits, then a real-day round trip, so 2026-02-30 is refused.
FORMAT_TOKEN_PATTERN plus month and weekday formattersformatWallClock calls date-fns format() with the enGB or nb locale.
LOOKS_LIKE_INSTANT, isCalendarDayText, inline copiesteamDate, teamDayText and renderDateText ask parseApiString / parseCalendarDay.
parseDateText, dateFieldOrderRemoved. Inputs are pick-only and TeamFormatDateAdapter.parse only accepts Dates.
asTeamDate falling back to parseISORemoved. Local date-time text goes through asLocalDateTime. Callers that built `${day}T00:00:00` pass the day itself.
The field trying every shapetoPickerDate(value, valueAs) reads only the shape valueAs promises.
TeamDateFormatter.format taking a day or an instantformat is always an instant; formatDay takes a calendar day.
Side effects to know Norwegian text from date-fns differs a little from the old formatter: "juli" for "jul", "ons" for "ons.", and "p.m." for a. date-fns reads browser-local getters, so a wall-clock time inside the device's own DST gap can print an hour off. An any-typed caller that passes date-time text without Z to asTeamDate now gets an invalid Date and a dev-mode warning. asTeamDate and teamDayText still accept either a day or an API date-time. Splitting them is the last piece of shape-guessing.
